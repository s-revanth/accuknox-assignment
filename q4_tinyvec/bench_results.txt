## 1. Unfiltered search: recall vs cost (200 queries, k=10)

       method  recall@10  dist evals/query  ms/query
-------------  ---------  ----------------  --------
exact (numpy)      1.000              8000      0.46
   HNSW ef=10      0.662               340      0.15
   HNSW ef=16      0.759               441      0.20
   HNSW ef=32      0.890               694      0.32
   HNSW ef=64      0.963              1141      0.57
  HNSW ef=128      0.994              1940      1.00
  HNSW ef=256      1.000              3171      1.81

Note: numpy brute force is one BLAS call, HNSW is a Python loop, so HNSW only wins on *distance
computations* here. In a compiled engine at millions of vectors that becomes the wall-clock win.

## 4. Durability

                                            operation        time
-----------------------------------------------------  ----------
                                    upsert, fsync=off  1.17 ms/op
                                     upsert, fsync=on  6.69 ms/op
recover: replay 8000 WAL records (rebuilds the graph)     14.59 s
            recover: load snapshot seq=8000, replay 0      0.03 s

fsync per write is the price of 'acknowledged = durable'. Snapshots turn recovery from
'rebuild the whole index' into 'load a file'. Batching fsyncs (group commit) is the usual fix for the former.
